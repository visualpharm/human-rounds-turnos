Sana Turnos funding and presentation opportunities

A ranked map of grants, investment, awards, and stages for an Argentine municipal-health platform built around patient access, responsible AI, and HSI interoperability. Verified on 29 July 2026.

Best path

Lead with public-health evidence, not startup hype. The strongest route is to win visibility now, measure a real Pinamar deployment, and enter the next Argentine funding cycle with the Municipality, the hospital, and a public science partner.

Priority 1

FITBA late-entry exception

Ask for an exceptional Line C entry now. It is the closest match, the highest potential grant, and the only opportunity where delay directly lowers the chance of admission.

Priority 2

World Summit Awards Argentina

Apply now under Health & Wellbeing or Government & Citizen Engagement. It is the cleanest current route to global presentation.

Priority 3

AI for Good Innovation Factory

Apply by 23 November 2026 for the 8 December 2026 online pitch and a route to the 2027 Global Summit.

Priority 4

SaludOnDigital LATAM

Request a demo or case-study role for 10 November 2026 in Buenos Aires. The audience already includes hospitals, government, startups, and pharma.

Investment is conditional. Sana Turnos is currently a philanthropic municipal project. Equity capital only makes sense after defining a separate regional business model based on implementation and support contracts. Patient data must never become the product.

Opportunity map

Order combines 3 factors: deadline urgency, fit for Sana Turnos, and likely benefit. Lower numbers come first. Closed or ineligible routes stay at the bottom unless an exception is being actively pursued.

What makes Sana Turnos fundable

Evidence package

Most strong programs fund evidence and scale, not feature lists.

  • Hospital and Municipality endorsement
  • Baseline and pilot comparison
  • Median wait and no-show rates
  • Administrative minutes per request
  • Referral error and recovery rates
  • HSI synchronization rate

Reusable application kit

Prepare once, then adapt the same verified case to each call.

  • 3-minute Spanish demo with English subtitles
  • 8-slide deck with one measurable claim per slide
  • Data-governance and consent summary
  • Open-source and replication plan
  • 10-month implementation budget
  • Letters from hospital, municipality, and science partner

Eligibility conclusions

Argentina has no high-fit national or provincial cash call still open today. FITBA 2026 closed on 20 July 2026, Merck-CONICET closed on 26 June 2026, CFI Innova closed on 26 June 2026, and Agencia I+D+i’s 2026 startup round finished accepting projects in May.

BRICS is not a current route. The BRICS Solutions Awards 2026 were limited to BRICS countries and closed on 10 July 2026. Argentina is not a member, while the New Development Bank funds infrastructure through members and public-sector channels rather than open startup grants.

UNICEF is real but structurally conditional. Its rolling Venture Fund offers up to USD 100,000 equity-free, but requires a private for-profit company, an existing prototype, open-source licensing, measurable public data, and a direct benefit to vulnerable children. A child-access module can qualify; a generic municipal appointment system should not be forced into that framing.
