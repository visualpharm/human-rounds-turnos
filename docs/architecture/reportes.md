- **Admissions ranking — how likely the patient is to show up (2026-07-27, product owner): `ranking_pacientes.py`.** A 0-100 score per patient for the admissions desk, with the two halves the product owner named: it measures the **share of appointments actually attended**, and it **must not reward booking junk appointments**. The formula is the posterior mean of a **Beta-Binomial** (empirical Bayes) over recency-decayed events: `puntaje = 100·(S+α)/(N+α+β)`, `α=π·k`, `β=(1−π)·k`, with π = the hospital's attendance rate (`TURNOS_RANKING_PRIOR`, 0.80 — from the 2026-07-24 meeting's ~30 % no-show falling to 18-20 % under the 31-day horizon) and k = prior strength in appointments (`_PRIOR_PESO`, 4). **Wilson's lower bound was rejected on purpose**: it starts a person with no history at 0, and "we don't know yet" has to be the hospital average, not the worst grade. Three properties fall out and are the point: no history → exactly π (nobody starts under suspicion); **volume is not merit** — the score converges to the person's OWN rate, so attending 30 appointments can never rank above that rate, only remove the doubt; and every event decays (`SEMIVIDA_DIAS` 365), so someone who missed appointments three years ago and comes reliably now climbs back with nobody having to forgive them by hand. Each fact carries **evidence mass `m` and success mass `s ≤ m`** (`PESOS`) — separating the two is what lets a cancellation be "half bad" without inventing an appointment that never happened: attended (1, 1); no-show (1, 0); **confirmed-then-missed (1.3, 0)** — worse than silence, the confirmation is exactly what stopped the slot being re-offered; cancelled ≥48 h (0.15, 0.12) — near-neutral **and deliberately NOT counted as attendance**, because if cancelling scored, booking ten and cancelling ten would rank excellent, which IS the "prize for junk appointments" to avoid; 12-48 h (0.5, 0.2); <12 h (1, 0.1) — in practice a no-show with notice; unknown lead time (0.3, 0.2) — legacy rows, benefit of the doubt. **`solicitudes.cancelada_en` is new and exists only for this**: `estado='cancelada'` alone can't tell three days' notice from twenty minutes'. Deliberately NOT counted, and these are decisions, not omissions: **raw volume** (a chronic patient booking twenty appointments a year is right to; waste is measured by REDUNDANCY, not quantity), **rejected requests** (the hospital's call — counting them would punish whoever's referring doctor has the worst handwriting, a poverty proxy), and **unmarked appointments** (nobody registered attendance = nothing is known). **A redundant appointment** = 2+ for the SAME service within `DUPLICADO_DIAS` (30) where at most one could be used (0.8, 0) — a legitimate series (ten kinesiología sessions in a month) never lands there because each ends in "attended", and an appointment that already counts as a no-show takes the WORSE of the two weights, never the sum. **The score decides nothing**: the web-channel block stays the hospital's agreed `TURNOS_NO_SHOW_LIMIT` rule, deliberately independent — a rule the desk can explain, against a score that informs; wiring it into anything (waitlist priority, for instance) is the hospital's call. Surfaces: patients list (sortable column «Asistencia», mouse AND keyboard — the desk includes someone who uses a screen reader; no number shown for `sin_historial`, since a repeated 80 reads as grades handed to people who haven't done anything), patient ficha (score + band + why + the line saying it blocks nothing), the admin queue's visitas dot/dialog, and «Pacientes de hoy» as a gear-only column named **«Historial»** — NOT «Asistencia», which in that table already means today's Atendió/No vino. Batch-loaded per page (`db_booking.booking_requests_for_ranking`, includes HSI-imported absences so the visitas dialog and the score never contradict each other on screen) and best-effort everywhere: no DB → everyone comes back neutral, never 0. Tests: `tests/test_patient_ranking.py` (properties, not magic numbers), `tests/admin-ranking-ui.test.mjs`, ranking cases in `tests/test_admin_patients.py`.
- **«Recorrido del paciente» counts only steps the database records (2026-11-07, product owner: "who got to which step… and so that we can configure it").** `app/admin_panel/reports_funnel.py` (`GET /api/admin/reports/funnel`, `?view=recorrido`): the unit is a booking request created in the period; the steps are pedido → aprobada (status or an `aprobada`/`restaurada` event, so a later cancellation still counts as «given a turno») → confirmada (`confirmation='si'`, off by default) → turno_paso (only requests approved now — a turno rejected afterwards never arrived) → presente, each counting the requests that pass it AND every earlier step that is on. Turnos past their hour with no attendance marked are the desk's action, not a statistic: the reason `sin_marcar` carries a line of its own with the count («Asistencia sin marcar: N. Se marca en Pacientes de hoy.»), linked for viewers whose menu has that screen (`fraseAbrible` in the client drops the link otherwise), next to the step and under the Gestión funnel (never a loss) (`_ACCIONES`/`_PANTALLAS` in `reports_funnel.py`). «Donde más se pierde» (revised 2026-11-13) names the transition with the most REAL losses (`perdidos`: rejected, cancelled, said no, did not come; a tie to the bigger share); pending, turno-ahead, hung (`colgada`: pending over 30 days) and unmarked-attendance requests are not losses — the first two get the hero line «Aparte: N siguen en curso», every reason stays in the step's list, whose sum is still the whole difference between steps. Filters: period, center, service, doctor (assigned, else the slot's). The set and order of steps is a setting in `catalog` config/`report_funnel`, edited from the report's gear (`POST …/funnel/steps`; at least one step on, unknown or repeated keys are a 400) — a new step needs a real column, never a side signal. Steps of the path with no record (site visits, a booking started and abandoned — expired holds are deleted —, signing up) are NAMED under the report, never estimated. Counts only, no PII; no export until the shared export component lands. Tests: `tests/test_reports_funnel.py`, recorrido cases in `admin-reports-disclosure.test.mjs`.
