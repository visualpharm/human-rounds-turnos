## 2026-10-23 · Patients on salud.pinamar.gob.ar get their account from the portal
Decided: `TURNOS_PATIENT_PORTAL_LOGIN=1` makes the municipal portal the patients'
only way in. The first portal request finds the account with the DNI inside the
validated CUIT (eight middle digits, leading zeros dropped) or creates it — active,
no password, the portal email — and records `X-User-Id` on it
(`users.portal_user_id`, partial unique index, Alembic `0025_patient_portal_user_id`);
later requests go by that id alone, like the staff login, and the account's DNI must
still be the CUIT's. Refused with nothing written: a non-person CUIT (only
20/23/24/25/26/27 pass), a disabled account, an account already bound to another
portal account. The portal email fills an empty
email only. Name and phone come at the first booking: Confirmá asks for the name
when the account has none. Patient screens show «Ingresar con el portal» instead of
the password, sign-up, recovery and DNI-photo forms (a refused visitor gets the help
line); the native patient login APIs answer 403. The reviewed bindings stay as a
pilot limiter (a list admits only its subjects; empty admits everyone).
Why: Product owner, 2026-10-21 (Teams chat with the maintainer): salud needs no DNI-photo sign-up —
the account comes from the portal with a validated CUIT and the DNI is taken from
the CUIT; DNI-photo sign-up stays a module for other installations. The portal team
confirmed on 2026-10-20 that only the gateway writes the identity headers, the app
has no public ingress and the CUIT arrives only validated.
Rejected: keep «never provision» with ever-longer bindings (the bindings were a
first cautious step before the gateway contract closed); key on the CUIT (the
portal names the account id as its stable key, same as staff); overwrite a saved
email with the portal one (the person's own choice wins, as with RENAPER).
Undo: unset the flag — the column stays harmless. · By: Product owner (product rule), agent
(shape, schema).
Source: `cuentas/portal_identity.py` (`patient_login_enabled`, `person_dni`,
`authenticate`), `cuentas/accounts.py` (`current_user`, `portal_entry_closed`,
`_config_core`), `core/db.py` (`user_by_portal_id`, `user_claim_portal`),
`static/platform/account-form.mjs` (`portalHtml`), `modules/turnos/service.mjs`
(`nombreSection`), `test/test_patient_portal.py`.
