## 2026-10-25 · A streamed static file's descriptors are read after the AnyIO worker lets go, not at `aclose()`
Decided: `test_native_terminal.test_stream_close_after_first_chunk_releases_every_descriptor` waits (polling,
10 s at most) for the fixture's descriptors to be released after `body_iterator.aclose()` instead of reading
them once. Starlette runs a synchronous body generator (`StaticFile.chunks()`) in an AnyIO worker thread; closing
the async iterator does not close that generator — the worker drops its last reference to it (`del item, args`
at the end of `WorkerThread.run`) only AFTER it has reported the chunk to the loop, and that is what closes the
three descriptors (root, `static`, `large.js`). With five lanes beside the database the loop thread reads them
first (3 left open, every run of the sharded suite); alone the worker is first. A real leak never settles and
still fails after the timeout. No product code changed: the descriptors are released microseconds later in
production. ·
Why: the check raced a thread it cannot see, so the sharded gate failed on load, not on a defect · Rejected:
a barrier job on the same AnyIO worker (relies on AnyIO internals); closing the generator in `native_terminal`
(product change for a test-only race); skipping the test · Undo: read the descriptors once again · By: agent
Source: `test/test_native_terminal.py`.

## 2026-10-25 · Data-subject rights, part 2: erase / anonymise / block (dry-run by default)
Decided: `scripts/subject_erase.py` (`core/subject_erase.py`) carries out an erasure request (Ley 25.326
art. 16). It only prints a plan unless `--apply` is given, and `--apply` needs a ledger request of kind
`erasure` for that person with a verified identity and a named `--authorised-by`. `anonymise` (the
default mode) deletes the derived and non-clinical data and keeps what may be the care record without
the person: the DNI-keyed identity moves to a random `ANON-` key first (`db.change_dni_in`, the
mechanism of a DNI correction), then the personal fields are emptied; steps run children first and a
final check rolls everything back if the person is still reachable. `block` deletes the same derived
data, deactivates the account and revokes its tokens, and leaves the care records under the person's
DNI. The clinical access log is never touched. It is refused while the person has live appointments, an
HSI intent still pending or an unresolved sync conflict. `db.change_dni` is now a thin wrapper of
`db.change_dni_in(cur, …)` (same behaviour) so the move runs inside the erasure's transaction.
Why: the privacy policy promises erasure «salvo lo que la ley obliga a conservar»; the spec of the
hospital direction (2026-10-24) and the owner's «todo lo que no es interfaz lo hacemos nosotros»
(2026-10-25). Which rows the law obliges us to keep is NOT decided here — see the MR's «Needs your
decision» and `docs/architecture/derechos-de-los-titulares.md` Q1/Q2: the rows that may be the care
record (interviews and transcript, consultations, health profile, imported history, bookings) take the
reversible side of the doubt (kept, anonymised), never a hard delete.
Agent calls (reversible): a ledger request is mandatory to apply (no erasure without a record of the
request and its identity check); the `erased` ledger event (hash only) is the tombstone to re-apply after a
restore; the lines other accounts hold about the person as a dependent are deleted (a NOT NULL birth date
cannot be blanked, and a guardian's declaration about an erased person has no purpose left); a
guardian's requests for a dependent keep the dependent and lose the requester's key; HSI mirrors are
deleted in `anonymise` (they would keep the person reachable by DNI) and kept in `block`; files are removed
after the commit and a failure is reported in the ledger, never skipped.
Undo: revert the commit; an erasure that already ran is not undone — restore a backup.
By: agent (the code); the legal classification of each row is the hospital's counsel's to confirm.
Source: `core/subject_erase.py`, `core/db.py` (`change_dni_in`), `core/subject_requests.py`
