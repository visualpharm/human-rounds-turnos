## 2026-07-30 · Country-specific integrations become connectors
Decided: every integration with an external, country-specific system is a **connector** behind capability interfaces (`connectors/`, `adapter_for(cap)`), swappable per deployment; a capability with no connector configured degrades instead of crashing. · Why: deploying in another country means writing new connectors, everything else keeps working. · Rejected: not recorded · Undo: not recorded · By: agent
