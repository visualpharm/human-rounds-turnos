## 2026-10-14 · KEK residency, format and key rotation
Decided: random 32-byte KEK, grouped checksummed base32, shown only in the
generating response, saved as an installation-managed TURNOS_ secret, excluded
from every canonical-env copy, never PostgreSQL. Wizard state carries only an
opaque token for expiring memory; commit persists secrets only at the end.
Immutable content header hashes bind chunk index/finality into GCM AAD;
a separate mutable envelope plus header MAC permits rewrapping without
reencryption. Each retained KEK has its own manifest ownership MAC.
Why: hashing mutable wrapped-key bytes into chunk AAD is incompatible with
rewrapping unchanged ciphertext. Rejected: passphrases/scrypt, readable
cookie-held keys and retaining KEKs inside the encrypted recovery bundle.
Undo: retain the v1 reader and original saved key for v1 archives.
By: agent.

## 2026-10-14 · A confirmed service save only wipes the doctors who ARE the service, and only at its center

automatic assignment, and `managed_services` — the doctor's calendar, capacity and
