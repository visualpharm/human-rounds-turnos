## 2026-10-17 · «Ahora no hay turnos» offers a date, a phone and the patient's case
Decided: the wizard's empty-grid step drops the franja and «A partir de» controls and
shows «Ahora no hay turnos.» with up to three options: (1) when turnos appear again,
computed from the booking horizon and the real schedules (earliest web slot beyond
today's horizon minus that horizon, with the opening time), or an honest «no new dates
loaded» when schedules hold none, omitted when there is no local schedule; (2) the
center's phone for a reason that can't wait, omitted without a phone; (3) «Contanos tu
caso», free text stored in `booking_requests.patient_note` and shown in the desk's queue
detail; the hospital contacts the patient. The slotless request keeps
`preferencia = «Cualquier horario»`, so nothing downstream changes.
Why: Reference-deployment counterpart (hospital, 2026-10-17) still found the step confusing and asked for exactly
these three options. Rejected: keeping the franja next to the case text (two asks for
one step); inventing a date for services fed only by an external source. Undo: revert
this entry's commit (the `patient_note` column stays, harmless).
By: agent, from the reference-deployment counterpart's request.
Source: `patient_web/patient_site.py` (`_next_opening`, `_no_slots_info`),
`modules/turnos/service.mjs` (`stepFranja`), `alembic/versions/0021_patient_note.py`.

## 2026-10-17 · Phone menu sized to content; titles get more leading; phones never break
Decided: the burger dropdown is `width:max-content` with a 240px floor (was a fixed 272px)
and hangs aligned to the burger's right edge (16px from the screen on a phone, was flush
to the edge); the buttons inside drop their extra 4px top margin, so «Chat» → «Subí tu

hairline divider (human-rounds.css «COMPACT FOOTER») is the intended design; the
public-chrome e2e now asserts exactly one 1px divider (the footer's own, or the home
specialties strip right above it) instead of no border. `.claude/settings.local.json` is
in the repo `.gitignore` (it was only covered by the owner's global git ignore).
Why: Product owner's phone-menu feedback (tight title leading, gap between the two buttons larger
than the bottom padding, menu too wide) and a center phone wrapping mid-number at 1440.
Rejected: a fixed narrower width (breaks long account names). Undo: revert the commit.
By: Product owner (feedback), agent (values).
Source: `static/platform/nav.mjs`, `human-rounds.css`, `templates/index.html`,
`patient_web/patient_site.py`, `test/e2e/public-chrome.e2e.mjs`, `.gitignore`.
