# Maintainers

Human Rounds is owned and stewarded by Generated Media, Inc. Maintainers act on
behalf of the project within the authority described in
[GOVERNANCE.md](../../.github/GOVERNANCE.md); maintainer status does not transfer ownership.

## Current maintainers

- **Ivan Boyko — lead maintainer.** Product direction, technical direction,
  release coordination and final resolution when consensus is not available.
- **Pavel Malai — maintainer.** Architecture and implementation review,
  contribution review and technical collaboration.

## Reachability

Use the public repository's issue and contribution channels for ordinary
project work. Report vulnerabilities only through the private process in
[SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md). Do not put patient data, credentials or private
deployment information in a public issue.

## Scope

Maintainers review contributions, preserve project architecture and licensing,
keep automated gates meaningful, and coordinate releases. They do not promise
individual support, uptime, clinical supervision or a response deadline. Those
expectations are defined in [SUPPORT.md](../../.github/SUPPORT.md).

Generated Media, Inc. retains authority over ownership, trademarks and
commercial agreements. The lead maintainer retains the final project decision
role described in `GOVERNANCE.md`.

## Emeritus

A maintainer who steps down may be listed as emeritus with their consent.
Emeritus status recognizes prior service but carries no review, security,
support or release obligation. Generated Media, Inc. updates this file when a
maintainer joins, changes scope or leaves.
