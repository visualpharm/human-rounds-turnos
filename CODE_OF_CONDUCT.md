# Code of Conduct

Human Rounds adopts Contributor Covenant 2.1 for participation in its public
community. This policy covers contributors, maintainers and anyone officially
representing the project.

## Our standard

We want an open, welcoming, diverse and healthy community where everyone can
participate without harassment. Expected behavior includes empathy, respect for
different experiences, constructive feedback, responsibility for mistakes and
attention to the good of the whole community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, insults,
trolling, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment,
publishing another person's private information without permission, and other
conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional community.

This standard applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review spaces,
community channels and events. It also applies when someone is acting as an
official Human Rounds representative in another public space.

## Enforcement

The maintainers named in [MAINTAINERS.md](docs/policies/MAINTAINERS.md) clarify and enforce
this policy. They may edit, reject or remove contributions and may restrict a
person's participation when conduct is threatening, harmful or inconsistent
with this standard. Reasons for moderation are communicated when doing so is
appropriate and safe.

Enforcement follows Contributor Covenant 2.1's proportional ladder:

1. **Correction:** a private explanation and request to correct isolated
   unprofessional or unwelcome behavior.
2. **Warning:** defined consequences and a period without interaction after a
   violation or repeated conduct.
3. **Temporary ban:** no project interaction for a stated period after a
   serious or sustained violation.
4. **Permanent ban:** removal from project interaction after a pattern of
   serious violations, harassment or aggression toward a person or group.

Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers protect the reporter's
privacy and security and disclose information only as needed to investigate and
act. A maintainer involved in a report must recuse themselves; another
maintainer or Generated Media, Inc. handles it.

## Contact

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Use the repository's
private reporting channel — a private security advisory or a confidential
issue, visible only to the maintainers — and title the report `Conduct report`. Include only the information needed to
understand the incident. Never include patient data, credentials or unrelated
private information.

If the report concerns every current maintainer, state that clearly so
Generated Media, Inc. can appoint an independent reviewer.
